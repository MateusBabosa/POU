Feel the magic with this most unique and beautiful Egyptian artwork of Anubis and Osiris.

Isis was the daughter of the earth god Geb and the sky goddess Nut. She was also wife to Osiris, god of the underworld, and bore him a son, Horus. 

The papyrus art is of Osiris and Anubis who are central, complementary figures in Egyptian mythology regarding death, with Osiris acting as the resurrected King of the Underworld and Anubis serving as the jackal-headed god of embalming and guardian of the dead. Anubis is often depicted as the son of Osiris and, according to some traditions, helped Isis mummify him, later guiding souls to his father's realm.

Papyrus is a material similar to thick paper that was used in ancient times as a writing surface. It was made from the pith of the papyrus plant. Apart from a writing and painting material, papyrus is used in the construction of other artifacts such as mats, rope, sandals and baskets.
